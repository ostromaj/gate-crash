Controls
  • P1: WASD to move, E or Space to grab and drop
  • P2: arrow keys to move, Enter to grab and drop
  • Solo: either set works. Esc pauses.
The loop
  • Orders read like AND = 1: deliver a 1 that came out of the AND machine.
  • Load the inputs, wait for the chip to finish, take the output to the order window.
  • Wrong bit loaded? Grab it back out with empty hands. Spare bits go in the bit bin.
Bitflip
  • Touch him with a bit in hand and he flips it.
  • Touch him empty-handed and you bonk him away, which also unjams a machine.
  • Missed orders fill the Null Meter. At 100% the foundry goes dark.
